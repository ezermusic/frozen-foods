Abak Company Limited continues expanding its premium frozen foods portfolio — introducing new cuts, flexible packaging, and strengthened supply options to better serve every customer across Ghana.

The frozen foods landscape in Ghana is evolving, and so are the expectations of the businesses and households that depend on it. Restaurants want more precise cuts. Caterers need flexible pack sizes. Wholesale buyers demand consistent year-round availability. Abak Company Limited has been listening — and the response is our most comprehensive product expansion to date.

Abak's expanded product range — available now across Ghana

The latest product expansion introduces additional meat cuts, flexible packaging sizes, and improved supply options across all three of our core categories: Poultry, Fish & Seafood, and Red Meat. And through it all, the quality benchmark that defines Abak remains exactly where it has always been — at the very top.

👥 Meeting Diverse Customer Needs

No two customers are the same, and the new product line reflects that reality. Different operations require fundamentally different product solutions — and Abak is now equipped to serve them all:

  • 🏨 Hotels & Hospitality — High-volume, consistent cuts in large-format packaging for daily buffet and à la carte service
  • 🍽 Restaurants & Chop Bars — Specific cuts, portion-controlled sizes, and fast turnaround for busy kitchen environments
  • 👩‍🍳 Caterers & Event Companies — Flexible order quantities with reliable last-minute availability for events of any scale
  • 🏪 Retail & Households — Convenient smaller packs for everyday cooking, easy to thaw and use as needed
  • 📦 Wholesalers & Distributors — Bulk carton formats with competitive volume pricing and dedicated supply support

✅ Same Quality Commitment

Range expansion at Abak has never meant quality dilution. Every new product addition undergoes the same rigorous certification and quality assurance process as our existing lines:

  • ❄️All products maintained at –18°C through a continuous cold chain from source to delivery
  • 🛡️Suppliers hold HACCP, ISO 22000, and where applicable, USDA or EU export certification
  • 🇬🇭All imports comply fully with Ghana FDA requirements and standards
  • 🌍Sourced from certified international producers across Brazil, USA, Norway, Netherlands, and beyond

Cold Chain — From Source to Your Door

Every Abak product travels from international producer to Ghanaian kitchen inside a fully temperature-controlled supply chain — no exceptions, no compromises.

While the product range continues to grow, Abak's commitment to quality assurance, proper cold-chain handling, and customer satisfaction remains completely unchanged. We believe that variety and quality are not competing values — they are complementary ones, and this expansion is proof of that belief.
